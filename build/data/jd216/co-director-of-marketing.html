ABOUT WELFARE AVENUE

Welfare Avenue is a youth-powered registered charity in Montreal. We serve home-cooked meals and connect people experiencing homelessness and food insecurity with the support they need, carried by a community of volunteers. We champion leadership, partnership and networking to bring community members and organizations together around that work.

THE ROLE

The Co-Director of Marketing leads Welfare Avenue’s marketing and communications side by side with the Director of Marketing. Together you set the direction for how we tell our story: our social media, our newsletter and website, our campaigns and our brand. You split the department between you and lead the people who make the content, so that donors, volunteers, partners and the people we serve see one consistent Welfare Avenue wherever they find us.

It is a leadership seat with real ownership: you plan the campaigns behind our events and fundraisers, decide where our time and budget go, and grow the next marketing leaders on the team.

WHAT YOU WILL DO

STRATEGY AND CAMPAIGNS

  • Set the marketing plan for the year with the Director of Marketing, tied to our events, fundraising campaigns and recruitment
  • Plan and run campaigns before, during and after each event and fundraiser, with clear goals and messages
  • Spot trends and new opportunities, and test new formats and channels

BRAND AND CHANNELS

  • Keep every channel on brand with the Welfare Avenue Brand Guides: Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok, the newsletter and welfareavenue.com
  • Oversee marketing material such as posters, flyers and event visuals
  • Build and keep relationships with our audience, partners and media

LEADING THE TEAM

  • Share the leadership of the department: weekly marketing meeting, clear priorities and owners
  • Coach the Content Manager, designers and writers, and review their work with kind, direct feedback
  • Recruit and onboard new marketing volunteers with HR

PLANNING AND REPORTING

  • Track the marketing budget and spending
  • Read the numbers each month and report what is working to the Co-Executive Directors
  • Attend directors’ meetings and key events

WHAT YOU BRING

EDUCATION AND EXPERIENCE

  • Enrolled in or graduated from a program in marketing, communications or a related field, or equivalent hands-on experience
  • Experience in marketing or communications for a brand, club, nonprofit or company
  • Some experience leading a team or a project

SKILLS

  • A strategic thinker who can also roll up their sleeves
  • A sharp eye for detail, tone and visual consistency
  • Clear, confident writing; French and English is a strong asset
  • Comfortable reading social media analytics and turning them into decisions
  • Familiar with Canva, Meta Business Suite and scheduling tools

WHAT WE LOOK FOR

We hire for integrity, curiosity and follow-through, and for people who learn quickly. Experience helps, but we care more about how you think, how you show up for a team, and whether our mission matters to you.
