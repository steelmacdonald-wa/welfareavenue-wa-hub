About Welfare Avenue

Welfare Avenue is a youth-powered registered charity in Montreal. We serve home-cooked meals and connect people experiencing homelessness and food insecurity with the support they need, carried by a community of volunteers. We champion leadership, partnership and networking to bring community members and organizations together around that work.

The role

The Content Manager owns what Welfare Avenue publishes and when. You plan the content calendar for our social media and email channels, assign the work to our writers and content developers, keep everyone on deadline, and review every post before it goes out so it is accurate, on tone and on brand. You read the numbers each month and tell the Director of Marketing what is working, what is not, and what we should do next.

The role exists so that our designers and writers can focus on making great content, and so that the people we serve, our volunteers and our donors see one consistent Welfare Avenue wherever they find us.

What you will do

Content planning

  • Plan and maintain the monthly content calendar for Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok and our email newsletter, in the Welfare Avenue Hub’s content calendar
  • Line content up with serving events, fundraising campaigns, partnerships and announcements, working from the event calendar Operations shares and the campaign plans from Fundraising
  • Turn ideas from marketing meetings, the team and the community into planned posts, and keep a bank of ideas for quieter weeks
  • Plan the newsletter and LinkedIn calendar with the Communications Lead so written and visual content tell the same story

Leading the content team

  • Assign content tasks to the Writer and Content Developers with a clear brief, a deadline and the assets they need
  • Track progress, coordinate deadlines and deliverables, and unblock people early when something slips
  • Run a short weekly content check-in with the team
  • Give constructive feedback and help newer team members grow in their role

Quality and brand

  • Review every post, caption and email before it is scheduled for accuracy, tone, quality and brand alignment
  • Apply the Welfare Avenue Brand Guides consistently across every channel, working with the Brand Manager
  • Keep a cohesive, consistent social media feed and a recognisable Welfare Avenue voice in English and French
  • Make sure people featured in our content have given consent, and that photos and stories respect the dignity of the people we serve

Analytics and reporting

  • Monitor engagement, growth and reach across all channels using the Hub’s analytics
  • Compile a monthly report for the Director of Marketing: what we posted, how it performed, and what we will change
  • Use what the numbers show to adjust the calendar, formats and posting times

Content creation support

  • Write captions, draft posts and step in on content creation when the team needs it
  • Flag important comments and messages to the Communications Coordinator

What you bring

Education and experience

  • Currently enrolled in or graduated from a program in Marketing, Communications, Digital Media or a related field, or equivalent hands-on experience
  • Experience managing social media accounts or a content calendar for an organisation, student club, nonprofit or brand
  • Experience coordinating or reviewing other people’s work is a strong asset
  • A portfolio, or accounts you have managed, that we can look at

Skills and competencies

  • Strong organisation: you can keep many deliverables and deadlines moving at once
  • A sharp eye for detail, tone and visual consistency
  • Clear written communication; French is a strong asset
  • Comfort reading social media analytics and turning them into recommendations
  • Familiarity with Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and TikTok, and with tools such as Canva and scheduling or analytics tools
  • Able to give feedback kindly and directly, and to take it

What we look for

We hire for integrity, curiosity and follow-through, and for people who learn quickly. Experience helps, but we care more about how you think, how you show up for a team, and whether our mission matters to you.
