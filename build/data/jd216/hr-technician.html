ABOUT WELFARE AVENUE

Welfare Avenue is a youth-powered registered charity in Montreal. We serve home-cooked meals and connect people experiencing homelessness and food insecurity with the support they need, carried by a community of volunteers. We champion leadership, partnership and networking to bring community members and organizations together around that work.

THE ROLE

The HR Technician helps us recruit and welcome the volunteers who run Welfare Avenue. Working with our HR leads, you keep the recruitment pipeline moving: posting roles, screening applications, scheduling interviews and making sure every candidate gets a timely answer. Then you help new team members get started well.

It is a hands-on role for someone who wants practical HR and recruitment experience and enjoys talking to people.

WHAT YOU WILL DO

RECRUITMENT

  • Post open roles on welfareavenue.com, CABM, LinkedIn and campus boards, and keep postings current
  • Screen applications and resumes in the Welfare Avenue Hub and shortlist candidates with the team lead
  • Schedule and sit in on interviews, and send follow-ups and answers to every candidate
  • Keep each applicant’s card up to date on the Hub’s Applications board

ONBOARDING AND RECORDS

  • Prepare the first steps for new team members: accounts, welcome message, first meeting and their onboarding plan in the Hub
  • Keep team records and job descriptions current with team leads
  • Reach out to past volunteers and student networks when a role is hard to fill

WHAT YOU BRING

EDUCATION AND EXPERIENCE

  • Studies or interest in human resources, management, psychology, communications or a related field

SKILLS

  • Organized and detail-minded: nothing falls through the cracks
  • Clear, friendly written communication with candidates
  • Comfortable with Google Workspace, Slack and online tools; experience with an applicant tracker is a plus
  • Discreet with personal information
  • French and English is an asset

WHAT WE LOOK FOR

We hire for integrity, curiosity and follow-through, and for people who learn quickly. Experience helps, but we care more about how you think, how you show up for a team, and whether our mission matters to you.
