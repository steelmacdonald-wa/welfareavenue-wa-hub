ABOUT WELFARE AVENUE

Welfare Avenue is a youth-powered registered charity in Montreal. We serve home-cooked meals and connect people experiencing homelessness and food insecurity with the support they need, carried by a community of volunteers. We champion leadership, partnership and networking to bring community members and organizations together around that work.

THE ROLE

The Logistics Manager leads operational planning and execution for Welfare Avenue’s volunteering events, so that every event is set up for success and runs smoothly. You lead the logistics and operations team, make sure every event-critical task is done on time, and make sure each event has a complete, accurate itinerary in volunteers’ hands.

Volunteer outreach, confirmations and day-to-day communication sit with the Operations Coordinators and Assistants. The Logistics Manager brings the leadership, accountability and structure that let the team deliver well-run events, week after week.

You also partner with the Program Development team to put new programs into practice, use budgets responsibly and deliver on grant commitments. A big part of the role is growth: taking our servings from every second Sunday towards every week, and then to more locations in Montreal and across Quebec.

WHAT YOU WILL DO

TEAM LEADERSHIP AND EVENT READINESS

  • Lead the logistics and operations team so every event is fully prepared and well run
  • Set clear timelines, owners and follow-through across the team
  • Make sure event-critical information is complete and accurate ahead of time: roster, locations, timing, supplies, roles and back-up plans
  • Spot gaps early and get the team to close them before event day

ITINERARIES AND LOGISTICS

  • Send each weekend’s itinerary to volunteers in advance, with the schedule, locations, roles and what to expect
  • Keep itineraries in one standard format in the Welfare Avenue Hub and improve them from team feedback
  • Oversee set-up needs: equipment, transport, supplies and role coverage
  • Hold the team accountable for volunteer confirmations and group chats, which the Coordinators and Assistants run

PROGRAMS AND BUDGETS

  • Turn Program Development plans into clear workflows, staffing plans and timelines
  • Make sure budgets, including grant-funded ones, are used well, tracked and tied to outcomes
  • Deliver grant commitments consistently, with strong operational oversight

GROWTH

  • Improve how reliably and efficiently we run events
  • Build the systems and routines that let us move from every second Sunday to every week
  • Help plan new locations in Montreal and across Quebec with logistics models that scale
  • Track how events go and recommend changes that grow our impact without lowering quality

WHAT YOU BRING

EDUCATION AND EXPERIENCE

  • Enrolled in or graduated from a CEGEP or university program in logistics, event management, nonprofit management or a related field, or equivalent hands-on experience
  • Experience in operations or logistics coordination, team leadership, volunteer events or event planning (student clubs and community events count)

SKILLS

  • Strong leadership: you can hold a team accountable kindly and clearly
  • Organized and detail-minded, with many moving parts and deadlines at once
  • Clear written and spoken communication; French and English is a strong asset
  • Comfortable with a CRM and event tools; you will work in the Welfare Avenue Hub, Slack and Google Workspace
  • Able to work independently and as part of a team, and available some weekends and evenings, including in person at events

WHAT WE LOOK FOR

We hire for integrity, curiosity and follow-through, and for people who learn quickly. Experience helps, but we care more about how you think, how you show up for a team, and whether our mission matters to you.
