Welfare Avenue is a youth-powered charity fighting food insecurity and poverty through direct community action. We bring meals and essential support to people where they are, then connect them with the services they need.

Our mission

Solidarity, not charity

We lead with the person, not their circumstance. Everyone who comes to a serving is part of our community, and is welcomed that way.

Food is our starting point. From a first meal, we build trust and connect people with the support that can change what comes next: essentials, community services, housing and health resources, through the partners who provide them.

Our main focus

  • 14,000+meals served since April 2020
  • 250+servings since April 2020, in Montreal, Toronto and Ottawa
  • 4,500+holiday gifts hand-delivered in 2024 and 2025
  • 600+volunteers since 2020

Our best estimates from our records, April 2020 to September 2026, rounded down.

  • Food security mobilization

    Home-cooked meals, served every second Sunday in downtown Montreal by youth volunteers who cook, drive and serve. In Toronto, our team brings free food events to shelters and drop-in centres.

  • Dignified resource distribution

    Clothing, hygiene baskets and seasonal essentials, offered with care and choice. Our Holiday Gift Basket Drive hand-delivered more than 3,000 gifts in 2025.

  • Dismantling social isolation

    A meal is also a conversation. Many of our volunteers come back again and again, so the people we serve see familiar faces and a community that knows them.

  • Integrating the care ecosystem

    We build trust through food, then connect people with the services they need, alongside shelters and community organizations across the city.
