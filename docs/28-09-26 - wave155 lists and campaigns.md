## What it does

- **Lists** (`crm_lists`, the CRM's Lists tab at `#crm/lists`, in the rail under Contacts). A list is groups of conditions: every condition in a group must hold, and a contact who matches any group is in (AND inside a group, OR between groups, the same idea as Keela's filters). The fields: contact type, volunteer role, tag, email permission, has an email, served at an event (ever, never, within N days, at least N times), gave (ever, never, within N days, lapsed for N days, total at least, at least N gifts), city, language, source, added within N days, active within N days, opened or clicked a Hub email within N days, needs review, and a search that ignores accents (`crm_fold`). Each condition can be negated. A live list (kind `dynamic`) keeps its conditions and follows the data (`crm_list_members` evaluates it when read); a fixed list (kind `static`) keeps the people who matched when it was saved. The builder counts as you go through `crm_list_preview`: how many match, how many can get a newsletter, how many a volunteer call-out, how many have no email, and the first names as links to their pages. "From the Contacts filters" turns the Contacts tab's current filters into conditions. On the live data on 27/09/26: 497 volunteers, of whom 154 can get a newsletter, 380 a call-out and 40 have no email; adding "served within 365 days" gives 84 (71, 71, 10).
- **Campaigns** (`crm_campaigns`, the Campaigns tab at `#crm/campaigns`, in the rail under Email). One message to one list: a name for the team, the list, the kind (newsletter, fundraising or volunteer call-out), the sender, the subject, the preview text, a headline, the message (a blank line starts a paragraph), a button and its link, with `{{first_name|there}}` placeholders. It is rendered in the newsletter template of wave154 (the brand layout, the charity number, the postal address, why they got it, the unsubscribe link). Preview renders it in a sandboxed frame; Send me a test sends it to the caller's own team address or a test recipient; Send... runs a check first (who gets it, who the rule holds back and why, shared addresses counted once, how many days it takes at the cap) and asks to confirm. A sent campaign shows a report: progress, delivered, opened, clicked, unsubscribed, bounced, held back, failed, cancelled, and every recipient with their status. Stop cancels what is still waiting; Copy into a new draft reuses the words.
- **The volunteer kind in the rule** (`crm_email_permission`): a volunteer call-out goes to any working address that has not unsubscribed; bounced and complained get nothing, as for every kind. Newsletters and appeals keep the CASL rule of wave154 (express consent, or implied consent within two years of a gift or a serving).
- **The send queue.** `campaign_queue` (live mode only) writes one `queued` row per eligible address and a `suppressed` row, with the reason, for everyone the rule holds back. `cron wa-mail-pump` calls `campaign_pump` every minute while anything waits. The pump claims rows with `crm_claim_queued` (`FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`, status `sending`, a claim older than 10 minutes goes back to the queue), checks the rule again for each person, and sends within the caps: `mail_daily_cap` 100 emails a day in all (Resend's free plan) and `mail_campaign_daily_cap` 80 a day for campaigns, which leaves room for receipts and thank-yous. Every send carries the Idempotency-Key `wa-send-<id>`, so a retried call cannot send twice. When nothing is left, the campaign is marked sent.
- **Test mode still holds.** While `mail_mode` is `test`, a campaign can be written, previewed and tested, and the Send... check explains that it cannot go to the list until an admin switches the mode to live in the Email tab.

## Access

No new page: both tabs follow the CRM page (`wa_page_ok('crm')`). `crm_lists`: staff with the CRM page read and write. `crm_campaigns`: staff with the CRM page read; they insert, edit and delete only drafts; everything after the draft (queued, sending, sent, cancelled) is written by `mail-send` with the service role. `crm_list_preview`, `crm_list_preview_flat`, `crm_list_members`, `crm_list_sizes`, `crm_campaign_audience`, `crm_contact_match`: authenticated and the service role. `crm_claim_queued`: service role only. Anyone with the CRM page can send a campaign (Steel, 27/09/26); the rule and the caps apply to everyone. Security advisor after the migrations: no ERROR, and no wave155 function among the warnings (checked 28/09/26).
