## What it does

- **Sending from welfareavenue.com through Resend.** `mail-send` creates the domain in Resend and hands back the DNS records (a DKIM TXT at `resend._domainkey`, an MX and an SPF TXT at `send`); Steel adds them at Namecheap; the Email tab shows their status and checks the domain. The from address, the reply-to, the mode and the test recipients are settings (`app_config mail_*`, changed in the tab by an admin, every change in `change_log`).
- **Test mode until Steel says otherwise.** `mail_mode = test` (the default) lets email go only to `mail_test_recipients` (Steel's two addresses). Switching to live in the Email tab is the go-ahead for real sends. The `test` action never goes anywhere else whatever the mode.
- **The templates in the brand** (`crm_email_templates`, five to start: a test message, welcome for a new volunteer, thank you for a gift, a reminder before a serving, a newsletter shell). Each is inner content with `{{placeholders}}` (`{{name|fallback}}`); `mail-send` wraps it in the layout: the cream logo on the green band with a gold overline, cream body in Space Grotesk with Barlow Condensed headings, the gold button, and the green footer with the lockup, the charity number, the postal address, why they got it and the unsubscribe link (Unsubscribe on a newsletter or an appeal, Email preferences on a confirmation). Admins edit subject, preview text and bodies in the tab; a preview renders in a sandboxed iframe.
- **The CASL rule, in one place:** `crm_email_permission(contact, kind)`. Transactional messages (a confirmation, a reminder, a receipt) go to anyone whose address works. Newsletters and appeals need express consent, or implied consent: two years after the last gift or the last serving (CASL s.10(13), registered charity). Unsubscribed, bounced and complained never get a newsletter or an appeal; bounced and complained get nothing. Fundraising without consent (the registered charity exemption in the regulations) exists as a setting, `mail_fundraising_exemption`, off by default. `mail-send` calls the rule before every send and logs a refused send as `suppressed`.
- **Implied consent written onto the contacts** by `crm_refresh_implied_consent()` (never shortens a consent the team recorded), run once on 28/09/26 and every morning at 04:35 Toronto (`cron wa-crm-consent`). Result on 28/09/26: 260 contacts updated; 250 people can receive a newsletter today (98 express, 152 implied), 1,417 have a working address for confirmations.
- **The send log** (`crm_email_sends`: who, template, kind, permission basis, Resend id, status, opens and clicks, bounce detail) and one entry per send on the contact's timeline (`crm_interactions`, source `resend`, `source_ref send:<id>`, with a status chip on the page: Sent, Delivered, Bounced, Complaint, Failed). `mail-events` (Resend's webhook, Svix signature checked, one row per event in `crm_email_events`) moves the status, and a hard bounce or a complaint sets the contact's `email_status` so the rule stops the next send by itself.
- **Unsubscribe** (`mail-unsubscribe`): a signed link per contact (HMAC of the contact id with `WA_MAIL_SECRET`, generated in the vault), a small branded page with a confirm button, one-click POST for the `List-Unsubscribe` header on bulk mail, the timeline notes it.
- **The Hub:** the CRM's Email tab (`#crm/email`, in the CRM app's rail under Email): who can be emailed today (four numbers that open the contacts filtered), the sending card (domain, from, mode, webhook, the last 30 days, the admin actions, the DNS table with copy buttons, the settings form), the templates (preview, Send me a test for a test recipient, edit for admins), the recent emails, the rule in plain words. A contact's page: the permission line (newsletters yes/no with the reason, confirmations yes/no), Send an email from the Hub (template, preview for them, allowed or held back before anything goes, the test-mode notice), the status chip on the timeline. The CRM tour gained an Email step; the apps tour's last step names it.
