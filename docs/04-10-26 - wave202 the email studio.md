## How it works

- **A designed email is a `crm_email_templates` row.** New columns: `design` (the blocks), `one_off`, `audience`, `category` (the shelf), `lang`, `description`, `created_by`, `created_at`; the kind check gains `volunteer`. `html_body` is the studio's own HTML (tables, an inline style on every tag, so mail-send's `styleInner` leaves it as it is) and `text_body` its plain text: mail-send fills and wraps it like any template (placeholders, the brand layout, the CASL footer), so one row serves a test, a one-person send, a campaign (`crm_campaigns.template_key`) and an automation step. The design cannot live in `vars`: mail-send's `cleanVars` cuts them at 8,000 characters.
- **Writes go through two checked functions.** `email_studio_save(p)`: staff with the CRM page (not viewers or blocked); a new row gets `st_` and ten letters (one-offs sort 900, templates 300); changing one needs its creator or an admin (the starters: admins); a system template (no design) is never touched; change_log `studio_one_off` or `studio_template`. `email_studio_remove(key)`: deleted when nothing used it, switched off when it was sent, a campaign points at it or an automation names it, so the history keeps its words; change_log `studio_remove`.
- **One renderer.** `MAIL202` (`build/parts202/c_blocks.js`) draws the blocks, their text version, the placeholders, links and images, and carries ports of mail-send's `fill`, `styleInner` and `layout` so the preview is the email as sent (`styleInner(render(d)) === render(d)` is tested). Node loads the same file: `build/data/wa202_starters.js` builds the starters' rows (`--json`, `--preview`).
- **The AI.** One tool, `build_email` (subject, preview line, blocks, questions, notes), through the llm router (`x-wa-task email_studio`, `x-wa-caller email-studio`; route Sonnet 5, then GPT-5, then Sonnet 4.5, 8,000 tokens). The system prompt carries the voice, the facts rule (never invent a date, time, place, amount, number, name, quote or link; only the writer's words, the template's and the public figures; a [placeholder] and a question when something is missing; ask when two things disagree; no claims the writer did not make about how things go), the links it may use, the shape, the question rules and the coming three weeks written out (a model is unreliable at weekdays). The message carries the audience, the kind, the language, the writer, their words or the template and their details, **the Hub's own check of their dates** (d_checks' `datesIn` and `checkDate`) and the photos by index, uploaded ones as images. `fromAi` puts the photos back by index and cleans every block.
- **Two real calls before the release** (caller `test:wave202`, $0.027 together): the first built a clean email but called Sunday 11 October 2026 a Saturday and promised things nobody had said ("No experience needed", "we will confirm your spot"); after the calendar, the date check and the no-claims rule, the second asked "Did you mean Saturday 10 October or Sunday 11 October?", left the date in [brackets] and kept to the facts. The studio's own checks stop the weekday mismatch in any case.
