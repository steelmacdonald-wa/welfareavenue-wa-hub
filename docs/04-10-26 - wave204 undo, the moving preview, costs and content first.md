## What people get

- **Undo and Redo** in the editor's bar, with Cmd/Ctrl+Z, Shift+Cmd/Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y. Every change to the email is a step: a block added, moved, copied or removed, a field typed in (one step per burst of typing in a field, until a pause of 1.5 seconds or another field), a photo, a moving preview, the settings, the Hub's own changes. The history belongs to the open email. The keys are the studio's when the email has the focus (or nothing does, or the preview does); in the Ask the Hub box, the answers and the send panel the browser's own undo works. When the Hub put the email together from your words or a template, **What you wrote** (or **Your details**) in the bar goes back to that step with everything you gave it; the email stays in Recent emails.
- **The moving preview** on the video card. Gmail and Outlook do not play video inside an email (Apple Mail is about the only one that does), but they play an animated picture. Make one from a video file: pick where it starts (a slider, or "Start where the video is paused"), 2 to 5 seconds, Wide, Square or Tall, Standard (up to 528 pixels wide, 10 frames a second) or Lighter (8 a second, smaller). The Hub reads the video in the browser, draws our gold play button on every frame, makes an animated GIF (one palette for the clip with our gold, green and cream kept exact; each frame carries only what changed) and shows its size ("Light enough for any inbox", or heavy above 2.5 MB). Use this preview uploads only the GIF, to `content-images/email/` like a photo; the video file never leaves the computer. In the email the preview sits in the green card above the title, the whole card links to the video, it plays as soon as the email opens and Outlook on Windows shows its first frame (play button included). The Hub's AI never sees the preview, and its changes keep it.
- **Text without the markup.** The front page says where to drop your text: Tell the Hub designs the email, no HTML needed. Text pasted from a Google Doc, Word or an email keeps its bold, links, paragraphs and points (turned into the studio's `**bold**` and `[words](link)`), in the editor's text fields and in Tell the Hub's and the template's own boxes. B and Link buttons (Cmd/Ctrl+B and K) mark selected words without typing the symbols; Link leaves `https://` selected, ready for the address.
- **What it costs.** Every AI button shows "about 2¢" and every by-hand one "free". Each email records what the Hub's work on it has cost (`design.ai`: calls and US dollars, from the llm router's `_wa.cost`; saved with the email, never undone, not carried into copies), shown under Ask the Hub to change it. The front page's **What it costs** panel lists each step (about 2¢ a call, a typical Tell the Hub email about 5¢, anything by hand free), the studio's spend this month, last month and in 90 days (`email_studio_costs()`), what the emails here now have cost (yours among them), and that sending is separate (Resend's free plan, 3,000 a month and 100 a day).
- **Your content first.** Tell the Hub needs at least 40 words of the writer's own (links not counted), with a meter under the box (the words, a date or day, a link); a thinner ask is refused before any AI call, in plain words that end "Bad input, bad output". Fill it in with the Hub needs every detail the template asks for (a template with nothing to fill: 20 words of your own). The front page, Tell the Hub's side panel, the template page, What it costs and the tour say it: the Hub shapes what you bring; it does not write the email for you. The prompt gains a Content rule: build from the writer's content, keep their words, never pad thin input, ask instead. Building by hand stays open to everyone.

## How it works

- `l_history.js`: `histSnap` (the email without `design.ai`), `histRecord(field)` from `changed(structural, field)`, `histGo(dir)`, the bar's buttons, `histKeyOk` (which focus the keys belong to), `backBtn` and `backToStart` (`SS.back` holds what was given; `SS.startNote` shows once on the page you went back to).
- `m_gif.js`: `GIF204.encode(frames, w, h, delay, {keep, thr, onProg, yield})`, self-contained (node runs it for `test/w204gif.js`). Median cut over a 15-bit histogram with true pixel averages, up to 255 colours plus the kept ones, index 255 transparent; later frames crop to the changed area and leave unchanged pixels transparent (a pixel within a squared RGB distance of 300 counts as unchanged); LZW with omggif's code-size timing; NETSCAPE loop forever; disposal 1.
- `m_moving.js`: the panel (`SS.mv`), `openMoving` (the file read through a `blob:` URL, its length and shape), `makeMoving` (seek, draw, crop to the shape, the badge, encode), `useMoving` (upload, `b.gif`, `b.gw`, `b.gh`, `b.gkb`), `movingSlot` (the card's form), `keepMoving` (the Hub's changes keep a preview, matched by link, else in order). `MAIL202.render` draws it (`c_blocks.js`; a card without a preview is byte for byte as before); `clean` keeps the four fields; `designForAi` leaves them out; the checks warn above 2.5 MB and note the photo rule.
- `n_text.js`: `htmlToMark` (paragraphs, line breaks, bold by tag or weight, Google Docs' `font-weight:normal` wrapper, links, list points kept on their line), the paste handler, `fmtApply`, `fmtBar`.
- `o_costs.js`: `money`, `aboutStep`, `addCost`, `spent` (a call that changed nothing still counts), `loadCosts` (`email_studio_costs()`; admins fall back to reading `ai_calls` themselves), the chip and the panel. `aiCall` returns the call's cost.
- `p_content.js`: `ownWords`, `contentFacts`, the meter, `contentShort`, `tplShort`; `e_ai.js` adds the Content rule to `aiSystem`.
- `email_studio_costs()`: security definer, staff with the CRM page (the test `email_studio_save` uses), totals only (`month`, `last_month` in Toronto time, `d90`); a board viewer is refused (checked on the live database).
- `build/site/_headers`: `media-src 'self' blob:` on `/` and `/index.html` only (the Hub's `default-src 'self'` otherwise blocks a `blob:` video). Pulled first from site_files (the local copy lacked the later `/i/*` section), so the deployed file is the source.
