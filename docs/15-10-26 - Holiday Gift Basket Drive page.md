# 15/10/26 - The Holiday Gift Basket Drive page (live)

## What was asked
At the fundraising meeting (15/10/26, Amanda Zoldak's team), Steel promised: "I'll build it, I might have it up tonight or tomorrow morning", then adjust it with Amanda's photos and copy by noon on 16/10. Amanda's asks: one landing page for the drive with what it is, the goal and last year's photos; a donate form "a couple times" on the page, through Stripe, without leaving the site; a progress display ("people are more likely to donate if they see others have"); and one link that Welfare Avenue, Scattered and the One Bag Challenge all send people to, so every drive gift lands under one tag. Three ways to get involved (her sponsorship package): in-kind donations, monetary donations, volunteering at the gift-wrapping event. Clothing may be gently used; hygiene essentials and toys must be brand new. Donations secured mid to end of November; the wrapping event's dates are not set (one or two weekends in December). Last year about 3,000 gifts; this year's goal 6,000.
