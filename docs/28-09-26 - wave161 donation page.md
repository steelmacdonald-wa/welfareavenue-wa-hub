# 28/09/26 - wave161: the donation page (the Hub's own online giving on Stripe, built and kept off)

Keela's donation form is the last public piece of Keela on the website. Steel chose to take gifts through the Hub on Welfare Avenue's own Stripe account (the account Keela's payments already use), added a restricted live key himself, and asked for the page to be built with as few paid tests as possible (more than three and stop). No paid test was made: everything below was checked with free Stripe calls, signed test events inside the database, and pages driven offline. The page is off until an admin puts it live.

## What it does

- **The page** (`hub.welfareavenue.com/give`): the brand look (the lockup, Barlow Condensed and Space Grotesk), Give once or Give monthly, four amounts for each (once $25, $50, $100, $250 with $50 chosen; monthly $10, $20, $35, $50 with $20 chosen) and Other amount, where the gift goes (asked only when there is more than one choice), in honour or in memory of someone (with the name), Add $x to help cover the card fee, the news box (never ticked in advance: the donor's express consent, with its words kept), the total, and Continue to payment. It checks the choices before sending, shows the server's errors in place, and waits until its token is old enough. The footer has the charity's name, number, address and email, and how the details are used.
- **While it is off** the page says online giving opens soon and gives info@welfareavenue.com; `?preview=1` shows the whole page to the team with a Preview banner and sends nothing.
- **Payment on Stripe Checkout, inside the page** (since wave161b, below): one-time (a payment, with Stripe's Donate button) or monthly (a subscription), Canadian dollars only (no other currency offered), the billing address required (a receipt needs it), card or a digital wallet (Apple Pay, Google Pay and Link where the device has them). The card fee is worked out on the server at 2.9% + $0.30 (`donate_fee_percent`, `donate_fee_fixed`), never taken from the page: $1.81 on $50. A session expires after an hour.
- **The thank-you** (`/give/thanks`): the donor's first name, once or monthly, the amount, the thank-you, the receipt note and, for monthly gifts, how to change or stop them. A bank debit that has not cleared says it is waiting. Change, above Stripe's form, goes back to the choices; on Stripe's own page (the fallback), cancelling comes back to the page with "No payment was made".
- **What the CRM records** (through the webhook, once per event): the donor, found by email or created (source hub_donation, kind donor; blanks filled, an address only when the contact has none), the email permission when ticked (never re-subscribing someone who unsubscribed), the gift (platform stripe, the fee and net amount, the card, where it goes, the tribute, receipt eligible), a monthly gift with each payment, failed payments, refunds (while a gift has no receipt its amount becomes what was kept; after a receipt, a note says the receipt must be cancelled or replaced), and a monthly gift that changes or stops (with why). Only what the page started is recorded: Keela's own Stripe payments on the same account are logged and ignored (they come in through the Keela import). A new gift starts the gift automations once they are on.
- **Gifts, Online giving** in the CRM (`#crm/gifts/online`): the page Live or Off with the preview link and what is ready (the Stripe product, the webhook and its secret), Put it live and Take it down (admins; logged); this fiscal year's online gifts, the fees, the monthly total and the checkouts started; the monthly gifts (active, late, paused, stopped and why, a link to each in Stripe); the page's twelve settings (admins save, with checks, and unsaved edits survive a refresh); and the last Stripe events (admins). The gift sheet shows the Stripe details (fee, net, refunds, a dashboard link), gifts refunded in part or in full carry a chip, reports leave out gifts refunded in full, and receipts show the refund.
- **Leaving Keela**: the Online donations step now reads the page: in progress while it is built and off, done when it is live.

## The database

Migrations `wa161_donations` and `wa161b_donate_mode`. `crm_donations` gains `refunded_amount`, `refunded_at` and `plan_id`. New tables: `crm_gift_plans` (monthly gifts: the Stripe subscription, the donor, amount, status incomplete, active, past_due, paused or cancelled, where it goes, the tribute, the dates of the last payment, the next one, a failure, the end, and why it stopped; staff with the CRM page read, only the service role writes; it follows a contact merge), `crm_stripe_events` (every event once, with its status, attempts, result or error; admins read) and `crm_donate_attempts` (every checkout try: session, spam, refused, error or test, with the network address as a hash only; admins read). `crm_stripe_apply(event, extra)` applies an event once and returns an error rather than raising it, so Stripe retries; its helpers (`crm_stripe_contact`, `crm_stripe_gift`, `crm_stripe_plan_upsert`, `crm_stripe_on_session`, `crm_stripe_on_invoice`, `crm_stripe_on_invoice_failed`, `crm_stripe_on_refund`, `crm_stripe_on_subscription`) are for the service role only. `crm_donate_mode_set('live' | 'off')` is for admins, is logged in `change_log`, and refuses to go live until the product, the webhook and its secret exist. The page's settings are the `donate_*` keys in `app_config`, guarded like the receipt settings (admins only). `crm_gift_receipts_v` and `crm_report` know about refunds.

## wave161b (28/09/26 evening): Stripe's form inside the page

Steel, after trying the live page: "I'd rather it stays on the same site. If stripe can embed their part of the form that would be best." The page now uses Stripe's Embedded Checkout: after Continue to payment, the gift shows in one line with Change, and Stripe's own form (email, card or wallet, cardholder name, billing address, Donate) opens below it in the page; after the payment Stripe sends the donor to /give/thanks on the same site. It is the same Checkout Session (the same product, metadata, webhook events and CRM recording), made with `ui_mode` embedded and a `return_url` instead of the success and cancel addresses.

- **donate v3** (platform version 8): GET says `pay: embedded` with the publishable key when `STRIPE_PUBLISHABLE_KEY` is set and in the same mode as the secret key; checkout with `ui: 'embedded'` answers `{client_secret}`. Without the key, everything works as before (Stripe's hosted page). The API version stays 2024-06-20, where the value is `embedded` (Stripe renamed it `embedded_page` in 2026-03-25.dahlia).
- **The page**: loads Stripe.js v3 from js.stripe.com as soon as the live page has its settings (Stripe's own fraud checks like that), mounts the form with `initEmbeddedCheckout` (or `createEmbeddedCheckoutPage` if a newer Stripe.js is ever used), destroys it on Change (only one can exist at a time), and falls back to Stripe's hosted page if Stripe.js cannot load.
- **Headers**: `_headers` (now kept in `build/site/_headers`) sets the Content-Security-Policy per path: the Hub's policy is unchanged on `/`, `/index.html` and `/f/*`; `/give` and `/give/*` also allow Stripe's documented hosts (js.stripe.com and *.js.stripe.com, checkout.stripe.com, api.stripe.com, hooks.stripe.com, link.com and *.link.com, images from *.stripe.com). Checked on the live site: one policy per path, the Hub's byte for byte as before.
- **Steel added** `STRIPE_PUBLISHABLE_KEY` as an Edge Function secret (it took effect without a redeploy) and registered hub.welfareavenue.com under Stripe's Payment method domains, which Apple Pay, Google Pay and Link need on an embedded form.
- **Checks, all free**: two embedded sessions made with the trigger secret (one-time $51.81, monthly $20.91) to confirm Stripe accepts the settings, then expired; on the live page, headless through the sandbox proxy, Stripe's form opened inside the page on a desktop and a phone (Link offered on the phone), Change removed it and a second gift opened a new one, with no page errors and nothing blocked by the policy; each session was expired unpaid and its log row removed. `w161page` now has 31 checks (every load under the /give policy; the embedded view, Change, the fallback, the phone). No payment was made.
