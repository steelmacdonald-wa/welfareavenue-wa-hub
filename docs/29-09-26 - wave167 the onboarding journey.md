# 29/09/26 - wave167: the onboarding journey

Steel (29/09/26), as wave166 was shipping: "Make the onboarding interactive with our branding highlighted everywhere. Use photos and make it the best most educational onboarding possible."

## The plan is a journey

The plan in the Avenue Academy (#training/onboarding) opens as a journey instead of a checklist:

- **The welcome**: a photo from the Photo library (the team under the tent, summer 2024), the logo lockup ("Building pathways in our community"), a gold star doodle, "Welcome, {first name}" in the brand's display type, their title and team, their day, the brand line (Service is solidarity, not charity), a ring with how far they are, **Start here** or **Continue** (the next step: its week opens and the step shows, unfolded when it sits under a bigger step), and their lead one click away.
- **The avenue**: the weeks as five stops on a road (Get set up, Who we are, Your team, Look back, Grow), each with its photo, how many steps are done and a bar. The E that moves (the brand's device) stands at the week they are in. Choosing a stop shows that week; on a phone the road scrolls sideways.
- **Each week**: its photo and due date, "By the end of this week you will" (three goals), "Why it matters", the lesson that opens it (Start here), then the week's steps (wave166's plan, one week at a time; the videos still play in place and Ask for help sits under them).
- **Your people**: your lead from the org chart (with when they check in), our co-executive directors (book 15 minutes in your first 60 days) and your team, with their photos (their Slack or Hub photo, or their initials). Each opens their page.
- **Welfare Avenue in numbers** (2020, 20,000+ meals and food bags, 700+ volunteers, 3,000+ holiday gifts in 2025, 3 cities) and **Did you know?**, a moment from Our story (open ones only) with Another moment.
- **Celebrations**: finishing a week brings confetti in the brand's colours, the E and "Next stop"; finishing the plan says "You are onboarded!". Each is celebrated once. Reduced motion turns the confetti and the bobbing E off.

## The lessons (educational content)

Four Avenue Academy lessons open the weeks, each with photos from the Photo library (only the ones marked ready; team and volunteer shots, no close-ups of the people we serve) and a knowledge check; the step that opens each week ticks itself when the check is passed:

- **Week 1: Welcome to Welfare Avenue** (10 min): who we are with our numbers, how the onboarding works week by week, the tools (the Hub, Slack, Google, Loom, Canva, Ask the Hub), and how we work together (check in and out, answer within 24 hours, 16 hours a month and two servings a year, one meeting a week, August off). Five questions.
- **Week 2: Who we are** (12 min): our mission and vision, what we do, the eight charitable activities; our story from April 2020 to August 2026 on a line, with photos; a Sunday serving from the day before to the clean-up; how we speak (we say, we don't say). Five questions.
- **Week 3: Working with your team** (8 min): the teams and what each does, good team meetings, tasks and requests in the Hub, your first serving. Four questions.
- **Week 4: Look back, look ahead** (6 min): the quizzes, the self-assessment and the 30-day evaluation; growing with us; what we hold on to. Four questions.

The facts come from the Hub's key facts (Steel's answers of 26/09/26); nothing in them was improvised. The lessons can show photos now: wave122's lessons have a `photo` block (full width, with a caption and where to hold the crop) and a `photos` block (two side by side), pictures from the Hub's own storage only.
