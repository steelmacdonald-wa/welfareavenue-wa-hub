## What changes against what is live (wave271 Build 1)

- **The offer** (recruit-actions): "Hours a week" is a choice of 4, 6 or 8 (default 4) and "Onboarding manager" is required, defaulting from the position. Both are saved on the card (`offer_hours`, `onboard_manager_id`) with `offer_sent_at`; a database trigger then makes the `offered` flow. The offer email is unchanged.
- **The manager is asked for times** the moment the offer goes out: a Slack DM and an email from hub@ with their own link (`/o/<mgr token>`). The page shows their typical week (wave276's, set on the Hub's Home page; the default is Monday to Friday 9 to 5 with "Set your week on the Hub's Home page"), lets them add or remove one-off dates, greys hub@ meetings they are invited to, and takes two picks: the onboarding call (60 minutes, day one) and the 1-on-1 (30 minutes), at least 48 hours ahead. A reminder after 48 hours with nothing proposed; HR is told once; HR can propose from the card instead.
- **Accept confirms the hours**: "I confirm I can give at least N hours a week to Welfare Avenue" is a required box (`hours_confirmed_at`). Old offer links (no `offer_hours`) show Accept without the line. Decline is unchanged.
- **At Accept, in the background**: the roster profile (active, no start date, `start_source manual`), the Google account (root org unit, the fixed first password from the vault, change at next sign-in), Hub access at member level to the new address. A clash, no licence, the setting off or the password unset becomes an HR task on the card with the reason; nothing stops the hire's flow. The hire sees none of this until the welcome email.
- **The times**: after Accept the page shows the manager's proposal with Yes and No. Yes re-checks both times (24 hours ahead, inside the manager's hours, clear of hub@ meetings and other hires) and books; No shows the grid with the 10-hour minimum, greyed where the manager is not free (unless the manager is free for under 10 hours in the window, when the grid shows without greys), then a closing page. A second No goes to a person. "Availability: already communicated" on the card turns the grid off: a No then says the manager will be in touch, and HR or the manager enters the hire's times on their behalf. No proposal yet: "We are choosing your times now" and the hire is emailed when the manager proposes. A proposal whose times moved sends the hire to the grid and tells the manager.
- **Booking** by the hire's Yes, or by HR or the manager from the card (the override, allowed on an accepted new-path flow with no proposal open). Booking creates the plan (day one = the call), the calendar invites with Meet links, the manager's notice, and sends **one welcome email** from hub@ to the personal address: first day and 1-on-1 with Meet links, the login (new address, the first password, "Google will ask you to change it"), the setup rundown as numbered steps and as a page (`/o/<token>?setup`, English and French, with Done ticks the card shows), Slack by the new address, Canva (the fresh link HR pasted on the card before booking, or left out), DocuSign, the job description. If the account is not made by then, the login paragraph says "HR will send your login details separately". The welcome waits while the account step is still running. HR or the manager can edit the welcome text before booking; the template keeps `[first password]` and the real password is put in only at send, from the vault.
- **The card** (HR Center > Onboarding, and the manager's own hires on Home and their team page): Offer sent / Accepted / Booked, who proposed what, the hire's answer, the hours confirmed, the manager's week state, the setup steps with Retry, the rundown ticks ("N of 9 setup steps done"), the Canva field, the actions: Propose times, Book the times, Enter their availability, Edit the welcome email, Set the manager's one-off dates (HR and admins), Change manager, Ask the manager again, They backed out. The server is the lock: a plain member who manages a hire can act only on that hire's card (`wa275_manager_of`); HR and admins see every card. Start onboarding is not on the new card (Book the times is the override); wave271's cards keep Start.
- **The first password** (Steel's decision, objection recorded in the design): one fixed password, vault secret `ONBOARD_FIRST_PASSWORD`, set by an admin on the card (8 or more printable ASCII characters with a letter and a digit), never in chat, the database, change_log, step details, the card, the preview or logs; Google forces a change at first sign-in. The card shows only whether and when it was set.
- **People already on wave271's path keep it**: flows created before `onboard_v2_since` never get the at-Accept steps; the card's new-path actions and signed-in Propose refuse them ("earlier path"); their offer links and grid work as before. James MacDonald's live card gains only the google and hub runs on Retry (the live proof of the account step).

### What changes
- **The offer** records who pressed Send (`hub_candidates.offer_sent_by`, only when the offer was emailed).
- **At Accept**, after the Google account and the Hub access: **Google Groups** (Team org@ for everyone; the department's group; Grants for fundraising; Administration and Directors for a leader; toronto@ when it exists) and **shared drives** (Org's Files plus the department's drive for a member; every shared drive except Accounting and Board of Directors, plus the department's, for a leader; Content manager). A leader is a hire whose position title contains director, lead, head, chief or manager; HR can change it on the card (only HR or an admin may make a hire a leader); "Re-check Drives and Groups" re-runs both.
- **At booking**: the hire is added to their team's **weekly meeting** series (no email to the other guests); the **welcome email** is the rewritten text (first day, the two meetings, the login with the Chrome video, the personal Hub link, the documents block with the job description, the signed offer letter and the drives), sent **from the mailbox of the person who sent the offer** (fallback: the recruitment owner, then hub@) to **both of the hire's addresses**, with **staff copies** (the recruitment owner plus whoever HR adds on the card) that carry "Copy:" in the subject and never the password or the personal setup link.
- **The setup page** (`/o/<token>?setup`) has twelve steps with Done ticks: Google sign-in, Chrome profile (download links, the video), bookmarks, Drive and the shared drives they were added to, their Google Groups and what each is for, Hub sign-in (ticked by the Hub itself), Slack and the open onboarding channel, Canva, the Avenue Academy, the job description, the weekly meeting, and the phone: two QR codes, one for the install steps page (`?install`: Safari and Chrome add-to-home-screen, then the Slack app), one that opens the Hub.
- **Once they join Slack**, the hourly job adds them to the open onboarding channel.
- **The card** shows the four new steps with Retry, the leader line, Re-check, and the welcome copies with Add people. Nothing is ever removed by the Hub.
