## What it does

- **The receipt PDF is the brand** (receipt-pdf v2): the green band with the cream lockup, the receipt number and date on the right, OFFICIAL DONATION RECEIPT / FOR INCOME TAX PURPOSES in Barlow Condensed; two cards (Issued by: the charity's name, registration number, address, website and contact; Received from: the donor and their address); the green gift block (date received, amount, advantage, the eligible amount large with CAD); Description of gift (cash donation, how it came, the date; in honour or in memory of someone; a gift in kind's description and appraiser); a thank-you card with the one gold star doodle; location issued, date issued, the signature image with the signatory's name and title; the foot with the descriptor line and the CRA's name and website (canada.ca/charities-giving). English or French, by the donor's language (the donation page they used, else the contact's language). An annual receipt lists its gifts on the first page and continues on its own pages when they do not fit. Barlow Condensed Medium, Space Grotesk Regular and Bold ride inside the function (Google Fonts, OFL, subset to Latin, Latin-1 and Latin Extended-A, 87 KB); a name outside those letters still stops an official copy (issue by hand) and shows ? on a sample. Samples carry SAMPLE / EXEMPLE, cancelled copies CANCELLED / ANNULÉ.
- **Every CRA element** (canada.ca, "What information must be on an official donation receipt", read 24/09/26 and 30/09/26): the statement that it is an official receipt for income tax purposes; the charity's name and address as on file with the CRA; a unique serial number; the registration number; the location where it was issued; the date or year the gift was received and the date issued; the donor's full name and address; the amount; the amount and description of any advantage; the eligible amount; the signature of an authorized individual (the uploaded image, under the control of an admin, as the CRA asks for emailed receipts: a read-only PDF, kept by the charity); the name and website of the CRA. A replacement says which receipt it cancels and replaces; a gift in kind carries its description and the appraiser.
- **Email it** (Receipts, admins): one email to the donor, the thank-you and the official receipt attached as a PDF (`Welfare-Avenue-receipt-<serial>.pdf`), from info@welfareavenue.com through mail-send (action `receipt`), as a transactional message under the permission rule (anyone whose address works; a bounced or complained address is suppressed with the reason). The words are the two new templates `receipt` and `receipt_fr` (editable in the Email tab): the gift and its date, the receipt number, that each monthly payment gets its own receipt, the tribute, how to ask a question. Live, the receipt is marked emailed (the date shows beside it, with the send on the donor's timeline); while the Hub's email is in test mode, the same email goes to a test recipient instead and the receipt is not marked. Email me a sample (English or French) sends the admin the email with a SAMPLE receipt attached.
- **Online gifts by themselves** (the switch in Receipts, admins, only while receipts are on): every paid gift through the donation page (one-time, and each monthly payment) is receipted with the next number the moment Stripe confirms it (`crm_receipt_auto_issue`, after `crm_stripe_apply` in stripe-events), its PDF drawn, and the donor emailed, in the language they gave in. The work runs after the answer to Stripe (EdgeRuntime.waitUntil) and never fails the webhook. A gift that cannot be receipted by itself (no address on file, a used number) stays due for an admin, with the reason in change_log (`crm_receipt` / `auto_skipped`); an email that fails is in change_log too (`auto_email_failed`) and the receipt waits in Receipts with Email it. Both `receipt_mode` and `receipt_auto_online` are **off**; an admin turns them on in Receipts.
- **The settings** are filled (change_log, 30/09/26): Welfare Avenue / Avenue du Bien-Être, 8200 av Bloomfield, Montréal QC H3N 2H9 (the CRA registration letter of October 2023), Montréal, Québec, prefix WA- from 1 (WA-0001 onwards), signed by Steel MacDonald, Founder. ## The database

Migration `wa185_receipt_email`:

- `app_config.receipt_auto_online` (off), guarded like the other receipt settings; `crm_receipt_auto_set(on|off)` (admins, logged; on needs `receipt_mode` on first).
- `crm_receipt_make` keeps more on each receipt: the gift's method, platform, recurring flag and tribute; the donor's first name and language (`donor.lang`).
- `crm_receipt_doc` also answers the service role (the automation) and returns the contact, when it was emailed; `crm_receipt_sample` carries the same extra fields and the language.
- `crm_receipt_auto_issue(gift)` (service role only): the two switches, a gift from the donation page that is due, then `crm_receipt_make`; refusals come back as `skipped` and go to change_log.
- `crm_receipt_email_doc(id)` (service role only): what the email needs; `crm_receipt_emailed(id, send_id)` (service role only): the mark.
- Templates `receipt` and `receipt_fr` (transactional).

A lesson from the pull: the repo was read through repo-sync from inside the database (pg_net), and 973 `get` calls issued at once, with heavy updates joined on `net._http_response` (no index on id), stalled the free-tier database for about fourteen minutes until it restarted by itself (the unlogged response table came back empty). Issue pg_net requests in batches of at most 60 to 90, copy each batch's responses out and delete them before the next, and never join a large update on `net._http_response`.
