# wave288: the Toronto forms and the second consent box (built 06/11/26, not released)

Hub CRM slice 1, Phase 5a (design CRM-S1 v2, B.2 and items 29 to 34; Part A.2 and A.5). Built on 06/11/26 on its own branch, on top of wave281 (the CRM rules in the database) and wave283 (the CRM page and gift rights), both built and not released. Nothing is live. Release waits for Steel's yes, and wave281 goes first. The website pull request that swaps the Toronto pages' Keela embeds for these forms (steelmacdonald-wa/welfareavenue-site-frontend#7) merges only after wave288 is live.

The design calls this Phase 5a "wave285"; it is renumbered to 288 because 285 is taken by the live onboarding-pages wave (286 is the Volunteer Manager wave, 287 another session's), and its branch keeps the name crm-slice1-wave285.

## What changes for staff and the public

- **Toronto volunteers and students sign up on Hub forms**, not Keela. `toronto-volunteer` (the live Hub form, closed today) gets the Toronto settings, and three new drafts join it: `benevole-toronto` (its French twin), `toronto-student` and `etudiant-toronto`. All four stay unpublished until the release's last step.
- **Each Toronto sign-up reaches Tate as a Request** ("New Toronto volunteer: <first name>", type Toronto volunteer sign-up, Ops Toronto), without the email or phone, which are on the CRM contact.
- **Two boxes on the forms.** Email: "Send me Welfare Avenue news by email. I can unsubscribe at any time." Text: "Text me about volunteering. I can reply STOP at any time." (French: « Envoyez-moi les nouvelles de Welfare Avenue par courriel. Je peux me désabonner en tout temps. » and « Écrivez-moi par texto au sujet du bénévolat. Je peux répondre STOP en tout temps. »). A ticked text box is express text consent, kept with the words, the form and the date, for a new person, a signed-in person's own record, or someone in the CRM who has not said STOP, is not do-not-contact and typed the phone already on file (or had none). Anyone else's tick stays in the answer and on the timeline for the team to confirm, as with the email box. Replying STOP still stops every text.
- **Toronto sign-ups appear in wave274's Toronto Volunteers** (the Volunteer Manager's Toronto city) straight away, through the tags `toronto` (the volunteer forms) and `toronto student` (the student forms, which also marks a student; Steel, 06/11/26) and the custom fields `gta_area`, `days_available`, `age_16_plus` and `photo_consent`, which `crm_form_apply` already writes from the answers (tags added when missing, never removed; the latest answer wins).
- **Toronto people join Toronto.** A Toronto answer puts the person in the Toronto chapter, and their application in Applicants received says Toronto.
- **The form editor:** "City written on new contacts" is gone. In its place, a **Chapter** select (Montreal, Toronto, or None; on a volunteer form it also moves the serving dates to that chapter's servings) and a **Campaign** select (the Campaigns, or None). Under the email permission, **Also ask for text consent** with the words next to the box. The preview shows the second box. French: « Demander aussi le consentement par texto ».
- **A form's settings.city is no longer written on new contacts** (a city is a postal address; chapters now come from the form's chapter).

The answers show "Text permission" (« Permission texto »). A one-choice answer of `both` saved to `volunteer.roles` means cook and server. `docs/EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` records v10.

**The /f/ page** (`build/f/src.html`, `build/f/index.html` regenerated with `build/f/make.py`, not published): when the form's `consent.sms.ask` is true, a second box sits under the email box with its words, and the answer goes as the top-level `consent_sms`.

## Worth knowing

- **Tate is stored by display name, not by id (settled).** The brief says `default_assignee` is Tate's `team_members.id`. On live, `request_types.default_assignee` and `dept_requests.assignee` hold display names (the column comments say so, and the two live assignees are first names), and notify, hub-push and team-inbox find the person by name; an id would reach nobody. The controller settled it: the file stores the display name of the one active Ops Toronto member whose first name is Tate, looked up at release.
- **French wording drafts (E10).** The brief says to copy the French `benevole` form's labels for names, email, phone, days, photos, experience and support, but live `benevole` only has names, email and phone. The French labels for photos, days, experience and support, their help lines, the French thank-you and the three new titles are a draft for Steel and Tate, as is the student wording itself.
- **"Either" is cook and server.** The student forms' role choice saves `both`, which is not a role key. form-submit v10 turns it into cook and server (so the Volunteer Manager sees both, and a serving sign-up would wait as "volunteer" for the coordinator to choose). The student forms must not go live before form-submit v10; the release order above keeps them drafts until then.
- **Who the text box counts for (fix round 1).** As with the email box, anyone can type someone's email, so a tick is written as text consent only for a new person, a signed-in person's own record, or a contact who has not said STOP, is not do-not-contact and typed the phone already on file (or had none). Otherwise it stays in the answer (`consent.sms`) and on the timeline for the team to confirm. `sms_opt_out` is never touched.
- **Text permission in the Request.** The Request's details leave out the email, the phone and the email permission, as before; the new Text permission line is not left out, so Tate sees whether they may text.
- **The day in the text consent source** is the day in Toronto (`to_char(s.created_at at time zone 'America/Toronto', 'DD Mon YYYY')`), like the email permission's day: an answer at 21:30 in Toronto says that day, not the next one in UTC.
- **A stray chapter never breaks a form.** `crm_form_apply` adds the form's chapter only when it is a real `crm_chapters` code, and since the final review (M2) passes it to `wa209_volunteer_apply` only then too. An unknown code never reaches `volunteer_applications.chapter`: the application is filed for Montreal instead of failing (`test/w288sql.sql` case 12).
- **Old settings.city.** Forms that still have `settings.city` (the Montreal volunteer forms) keep it in their settings, but nothing reads it any more. Montreal people still count as Montreal: an application is Montreal by default, and someone with no chapter row is in the Montreal filter.
- **The editor shows Chapter and Campaign on every form**, not only volunteer forms, since the brief says a volunteer form also moves its serving location. Saving a volunteer form with a chapter keeps its serving location in step.
- **The live check switches off four triggers inside its own transaction.** Applying a test answer files a Request and an application, whose triggers (`trg_wa123_request_new`, `trg_wa123_request_update`, `trg_wa89_request_filed`, `trg_wa239_volunteer_applied`) call edge functions. `test/w288live.sql` switches them off before the answers and stops if any other trigger on a table the answers write could call out. Everything rolls back, so they are on again afterwards (checked).
- **Line endings on this PC.** Git checks files out with CRLF here, which changes the function text the PGlite fixtures hold, so `w281sql.mjs` and `w283sql.mjs` fail their md5 checks in this working copy. They pass on an LF copy of the commit; the committed files are LF. `w288sql.mjs` reads its files as LF, so it passes either way (checked on a CRLF copy too).
- **Not done here:** `docs/DATA_MODEL.md` is regenerated at the merge, with the other shared generated files.
