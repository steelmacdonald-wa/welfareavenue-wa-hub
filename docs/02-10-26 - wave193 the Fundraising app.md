## The scanner (grant-scan v3)

A run works in steps because one invocation lives 150 seconds here (measured: the first two test runs were cut off at 150 s). Each step does about a minute of work in the background and calls the function again with `{action: 'continue'}`; a source counts as read for the run once its `last_checked` is after the run's start. Pages are fetched four at a time, hashed, and read by `grant_extract` only when they changed; one web search a step: DeepSeek with OpenRouter's `:online` plugin (Exa results, about $0.01 a question) answers and cites its pages, and the extract route records the calls from those pages. Why not Sonnet's or GPT's own search: one question cost $0.72 to $0.78 (245K to 323K input tokens) on 02/10/26. Finds are matched to the funders (name, aliases) and the grants (program overlap or the same deadline): known, duplicate (the run, or the last 90 days) or new. The last step posts the new finds scoring at least 55 to #grants (`fund_settings.scanner`), unless the run is a test. Cron 88 `wa-grant-scan`, Mondays 13:30 UTC (09:30 Toronto in the fall); Scan now from the app.

The test run of 02/10/26 (run 3, a test, nothing posted): 62 pages checked, 5 changed, 8 searches, 13 new finds (Mission inclusion's 2026 call and its housing program, Methanex, and ten weak ones under 55), 20 known or repeated, $0.18 in model calls, 9 steps, 6 minutes, 16 problems: 15 pages the function cannot fetch (403s from Imagine Canada, Right To Food, 3CA, Sobeys, Starbucks and Canva; 404s from CharityVillage, the City of Toronto and Fondation Sibylla Hesse; TLS and HTTP/2 errors from ISED, Second Harvest, BMO and Home Depot; Canada Post 500; Walmart timed out) and one search that took longer than 75 s. Those pages need a better address or a browser; the list is in the funders' sheets (what the scanner reads).

## The writer (grant-write v1)

`draft` builds the prompt from the fact base (verified facts used as written; needs_check with a warning; conflict figures never stated, a [confirm: …] placeholder instead), the playbook's sections 2, 4 and 6, the grant and funder's context, and up to five documents (the answer bank, the same funder's files, the closest by full-text search), and asks Sonnet for the answers with the `write_answers` tool (facts, refs and warnings per answer, a consistency list and a reviewer's note). `review` reads the draft as a program officer (`review_answers`). `fit` runs the playbook's screen (`score_fit`: the gates, seven dimensions 0 to 5 with weights 15/20/10/15/15/10/15, the verdict, the first step, the hours). Everything goes through the llm router; the function sends nothing anywhere.
