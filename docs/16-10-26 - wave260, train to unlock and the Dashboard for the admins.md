## 2. Train to unlock

- A page whose id is in `app_config.page_gate_map` (built-in copy `MAP_DEFAULT` in `src/260_wave260.seg`) and whose tour (`trainings` `tour:<key>`, active, a `w122.tours` entry the person can take) is not done shows a lock card in place of the page: the page's name, its line (the tour's description), "Take the n-minute tour to unlock it" (the tour's minutes), Start the tour, Back to Home (or the Board overview).
- Start the tour shows the page and runs its tour on it (wave122's engine, `w122.startTour`); finishing it records completion exactly as from the Academy (`complete()`), and the page stays open from then on. Stopping midway (Esc, the x) locks the page again. wave122 now fires `w122tour` events (start, end, finish) and exports `isDone`, `me`, `minutesOf`.
- Locked pages carry a small lock in the menu (`data-w260lock` on the tab, drawn in CSS) and still open (to the card). Every arrival goes through `showPanel`, so menu clicks, links, notifications and deep links follow the same rule.
- Never locked: Home, the Avenue Academy, people pages, Needs me, the sign-in and the must-answer pop-ups (they sit above the page, so the order on load stays Welcome back, meeting attendance, tasks and due dates, then the page lock), any page without an active tour, and any page whose tour the person cannot take. Admins never see a lock. Directors on the board view (gmail accounts) are never asked: they cannot read the Academy's trainings.
- Important info is locked like any other page: it is not an onboarding step (no onboarding template, journey or HR setup step points at it).
- Pages and their tours: overview, dashboard; team pages (team, opsmtl, progdev, opsto, marketing, fundraising, hr, board) to `team`; requests, calendar, brain, transcripts, agendas, hubmail, orgchart, volunteering, hrcentre, finances, contentcal, socialstats, create, importantinfo, photos, activity, adminpanel, fundevents, governance, committees, bank, ourstory, partners, documents, crm, fund, grants, orggoals, projects, goalmap; studio to `contentstudio`; projectpage to `project`. Sub-pages share their page's tour (the Volunteer Manager's Phone, Calendar, Sign-offs and texts; the CRM's Email studio; the Strategy Library; the HR walkthroughs are not required).
- Admin, Page tours (`#adminpanel/tours`, the Admin centre's rail): Train to unlock on or off (`page_gate`), Who opens the Executive dashboard (`dashboard_people`), Let someone through (`page_gate_exempt`, also per person in the table), the count of people who will see a lock and the pages most of them still have to tour, and every person against every page tour (toured, locked, or a page they cannot open, read from the access profiles). Reads `wa260_page_tours()`, writes through `wa260_gate_set()`; both check `is_wa_admin()` inside and are executable by `authenticated` only; `wa_app_config_guard` keeps the four keys admin-only.
- Ask the Hub is unchanged: it can still answer about, and act on, the data of a page the person has not toured (the gate is on pages, not on data).
