## What it does

- **Custom fields** (`crm_fields`, `crm_contact_fields`). What the Hub has no column for gets a field: a key (`field:<key>` in a form question), a label, a type (text, long text, number, date, choice, multi, yes or no) and its choices. Nine were seeded: instagram, photo_consent, age_16_plus, gta_area (the seven parts of the GTA on the Toronto form), days_available, food_nonprofit_experience, support_note, rally_paper_team and donor_readiness, with 84 values carried over from the Keela references already on the contacts. The Forms tab lists them with their kind, the forms that use them and how many contacts have a value; a field can be archived and brought back. A contact's page shows them under "More about them".
- **Forms** (`crm_forms`, the CRM's Forms tab at `#crm/forms`, in the rail under Contacts). The list shows each form's kind, status, public address, answers (this week, errors) and, once live, Copy the link. The editor: title, web address (fixed once live), kind (volunteer, newsletter or general), language (English or French), intro, the questions (type, label, help, choices written `code | words`, required, order), where each answer is saved (the contact's name, email, phone, city, province, postal code, address or language; the volunteer roles; the serving dates as roster sign-ups; a custom field or a new one named after the question; the note on the timeline; or kept with the answer only), the email permission box and its words, the thank-you, and for volunteer forms whether an application also creates or updates the MTL volunteering record, which servings the dates question offers, the city and the tags. Preview shows the page as the public will see it. Put it live asks first and names the public address; the database refuses a form without an email question, and a newsletter form that does not ask for permission (`crm_forms_stamp`). Stop taking answers closes it (the page then says so); only a draft can be deleted.
- **Answers** (`crm_form_submissions`). Every submission is kept as sent: the answers, what was shown, what goes where, the permission words and whether the box was ticked, and what the Hub did. The Answers view hides spam and tests until asked; an opened answer shows every line, "The Hub: ..." (created or updated the contact, the volunteering record, the sign-ups, the fields) and Open their page; a failed one says what went wrong.
- **What an answer does** (`crm_form_apply`, one transaction, safe to run twice). It finds the contact by email (never a merged one). A volunteer form with the MTL record creates or updates the volunteering record (flagged for review, "Applied through the Hub form ... on <date>", roles and tags added), and the existing sync creates or links the contact. Otherwise a new contact is created (source `hub_form`) or the existing one only gets what it is missing: nothing the team entered is overwritten, and a phone is added only when the contact has none, so a form cannot put a number on someone else's record. A ticked box records express consent with the form's name and the day (CASL); a contact who unsubscribed stays unsubscribed even if the box is ticked again (anyone can type an address), and the tick shows on the timeline for the team to confirm. Custom fields take the latest answer. Each serving date picked becomes a roster sign-up with status TBD and the first role they chose, for the team to confirm. The timeline gets the answer.
- **The public page** (`build/f/src.html`, built by `build/f/make.py` into `build/f/index.html`, served for every `/f/<slug>` by a `_redirects` rule). The brand look (the lockup, Barlow Condensed and Space Grotesk), English or French, the questions, the permission box never ticked in advance, and the charity's name, number, address and email in the footer. It checks the answers before sending, shows the server's errors under the right question, fetches a new token and keeps the answers if the page sat open more than a day, and shows the thank-you. A draft or unknown address says "This form is not available"; a closed form says it is closed.
- **Keeping bots out** (`form-submit`, see EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md). A hidden field people never see, a token signed with `WA_FORM_SECRET` that must be at least 3 seconds and at most a day old, 20 sends per network address in 10 minutes (the address is kept only as a keyed hash) and 3 per email per form in an hour. A failed hidden-field or token check is stored as spam and answered like a success, so a bot learns nothing.
- **The three forms, all drafts:** `volunteer` ("Volunteer with Welfare Avenue": name, email, phone, roles, the Montreal servings, Instagram, a note; creates or updates the MTL volunteering record), `toronto-volunteer` ("Volunteer in Toronto": the contact only, tagged toronto; part of the GTA, 16 or older, photos, days, experience, the support note) and `newsletter` ("Get the Welfare Avenue newsletter": name, email, language; permission required).
- **Merging contacts** now also moves custom fields (the kept contact's value wins where both have one), form submissions and the email send log.

## Access

No new page: the Forms tab follows the CRM page (`wa_page_ok('crm')`). `crm_fields` and `crm_contact_fields`: staff with the CRM page read and write. `crm_forms`: staff with the CRM page read and edit; they insert only drafts and delete only drafts. `crm_form_submissions`: staff with the CRM page read; only `form-submit` writes, with the service role. `crm_form_apply`: service role only. `crm_forms_stamp`: trigger only (execute revoked). The anonymous role has no grant on any of these tables: the public page only talks to `form-submit`.
