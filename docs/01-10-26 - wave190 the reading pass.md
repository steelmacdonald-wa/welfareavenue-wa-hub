## What the audit found

- **The Avenue Academy did not use the brand's type.** Wave122 was built in the system font (the "Apple feel"), while the rest of the Hub reads in Space Grotesk with Barlow Condensed titles (wave114). The onboarding journey (wave167) was on brand, but every lesson and reading it opened was not.
- **The nine Using the Hub modules had become walls of text.** Each wave added a sentence, so module 3 was a 320-word paragraph, module 7 a 284-word paragraph and module 1 a 248-word paragraph. Module 6 still described Volunteering as a page (it has been the Volunteer Manager app since wave176), module 1's apps left the Volunteer Manager out, and module 7 still named ClickUp.
- **The readings imported from the Slack training lists and ClickUp** drew badly. Numbered steps lost their numbers and their points, bold lines showed as text, and one link was cut off by "[…]". There were empty steps ("3."), "Preserved from ClickUp" footers on ten trainings, a dead ClickUp link on training 22, and a placeholder with no content (34). One reading told people to share login credentials (35).
- **The master onboarding mixed four ways of saying what to do:** "**To complete the task:**", "To complete this task:", "Mini task:" and "Link to quiz:". It also had:
  - the Hub's old address in the step The Hub;
  - the DocuSign ask twice;
  - "send one of your learnings (one from the video and one from the CRA website)", which asks for two.

  Step text was 12.5px and grey.
- **The week 1 lesson** gave the old address on its tools card, and offered "In ClickUp" as a quiz answer, even though no onboarding text may name ClickUp. The welcome email's template, the Welcome kit's fallback, the branded email and the invite message also used the old address.

## The page (waiting on Netlify, see below)

- **The brand's type in the Avenue Academy** (`122_wave122`):
  - Reading is in Space Grotesk (`SF`).
  - Titles are Barlow Condensed (`DISP`), in capitals and the green: the hero, the sections, the onboarding cards, a sheet's title, a chapter's title, a reading's headings, the path hero, the quiz result, the celebration, a tour's card and the certificate.
  - A chapter's title has the gold rule under it, and a lesson's numbers are in the display face.
  - The lead is green, and bullets are gold.
- **Readings read like a page** (`articleText`, `articleBlock` in 122). A reading, or a video's or a document's description:
  - A block that is only `**bold**`, or a line starting `##`, is a heading. A bold line inside a block is a small heading.
  - `1.` lines are numbered steps, drawn as green circles; the `- ` lines under one are its points. Numbering carries on after a paragraph.
  - A paragraph of text with a list under it draws as both.
  - `Tip:`, `Good to know:`/`Note:` and `Watch out:` are callouts, as `Try it:` and `Done when:` were. A callout's text starts with a capital.
  - Text written before reads as it did, except that numbered lists now show their numbers.
  - The Add a training form says how to write a reading.
- **Onboarding steps read better** (`190_wave190`, which wraps `wa37Md`):
  - "To complete this step:" (and the older wordings) becomes a gold box titled TO COMPLETE THIS STEP.
  - An all-italic line becomes a quiet note, for how the step ticks itself.
  - Step text is 13.5px and darker everywhere a step is drawn: the plan, the journey, the preview, and Updated since you finished.
- **The old address** (`046`, `108`, `113`, `131`): the Welcome kit, the branded email and the invite message say hub.welfareavenue.com.
