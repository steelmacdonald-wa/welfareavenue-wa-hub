# 30/09/26 - wave173: cooks say how their food reaches us

Steel (30/09/26): "cooks need to confirm their pick up address and ensure it's in the pick up areas seen on our old website (there should be a list of neighborhoods) or drop off at our serving location. If they drop off they could come serve as well so we'd just need to know that. Also for the questions what dish would you like to cook, it should what would prefer to cook."

- **The areas:** the old website's "Please Note" box, kept as a screenshot in the SOP step "Call cooks to Confirm Availability, Pickup or Drop Off": All downtown Montreal districts, Town of Mount Royal, Le Plateau, Mile End, Outremont, Westmount, Laurier, Saint-Henri, Parc-Extension, Griffintown, Mont-Royal, Villeray, Côte-des-Neiges, The Village, Little Italy. Cooks outside them drop their food off at 1500 Rue Berri, Montréal, QC H2L 2C4 by 12:00 pm. (The old site's form also checked postal codes against a list of about 70 first-three-character codes; that list has codes far outside Montreal, so it is not used.) The areas, the drop-off place and time are app_config `cook_pickup_areas`, `cook_dropoff_place`, `cook_dropoff_time`, editable in Text settings.
- **The confirm page (cooks):** "Which dish would you prefer to cook?"; then "How does your food reach us?": Pick it up from my home (neighbourhood from the list or "My area is not listed", which says to drop off instead and cannot be saved as a pickup; street address, filled from their record; postal code, checked) or I will drop it off at 1500 Rue Berri by 12:00 pm (then: stay and serve, or just drop off). Saving without choosing asks first.
- **Where it lands:** `event_roster.confirm` (cook_location home or drop off, address, neighbourhood, postal_code, stay_to_serve), `event_roster.detail.pickup_dropoff` (Pick-up or Drop-off) and a pickup's address on `volunteer_people.address`, so the event summary's pickup route (wave31) includes it.
- **The Hub:** the cook's card says "Pickup: Le Plateau · 123 Rue Rachel Est" or "Drops off · staying to serve"; Text settings has the pickup areas and the drop-off.
